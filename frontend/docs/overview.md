# Project Overview

The AHCoF Member Mobile Application is a digital transformation initiative designed to move the Adventist Heritage Co-operative Fund (AHCoF) from a predominantly office-based service model toward a modern, secure, convenient, and self-service digital experience.

The application provides AHCoF members with access to their financial information and selected services directly from their mobile devices. Members can view savings, monitor loans, manage shares, review transactions, access digital forms, receive notifications, and eventually perform financial transactions through integrated mobile money services.

The long-term objective is to establish the AHCoF mobile application as a central digital channel through which members can interact with AHCoF without needing to physically visit an AHCoF office for routine services.

## Target Users
- Individual members(focus in current MVP)
- Church workers
- Retirees
- Institutional members
- Churches / districts

## Phase 1 Scope
Onboarding, authentication, dashboard, savings, loans, shares & dividends, transactions, digital forms, notifications, profile, mobile money deposit/withdrawal, and loan application flow.
